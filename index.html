The company

What TRADEX. is building

European vehicle trading brings together a decision about stock and a chain of work across borders. TRADEX. is building an exchange that connects the two.

Illustrative automotive photography

01

What we do today

TRADEX. is a European B2B vehicle trading company. We buy packages of young used vehicles from leasing and rental companies, fleet operators, importers and dealer groups. We make those vehicles available to professional dealers for resale in their local markets.

We coordinate the work around the deal, from vehicle preparation and loading to transport and paperwork. After booking, the dealer portal brings status updates, documents and invoices into one account.

  • Young used vehicles
  • Vehicle packages
  • Professional dealers
02

Why the exchange exists

Suppliers need an outlet when a volume of vehicles becomes available. Dealers need cars they can resell in their market and a delivered price they can put into their purchase calculation before they book.

Buying in packages connects those needs. It gives suppliers a direct purchasing contact and lets dealers consider individual vehicles against their own stock requirements.

03

What we are building

We are developing a European vehicle exchange that brings buying decisions and the work that follows into one process. Vehicle data and delivered pricing support the decision to buy. The portal shows the progress of the purchase once it is booked.

That process has a physical side. Our own compound and workshop support vehicle preparation, our logistics team arranges transport and a named account manager gives each customer someone to turn to.

The ecosystem

Who the exchange connects

Vehicle manufacturers, rental companies and other suppliers can discuss stock with our purchasing team. Dealers and other professional buyers enter the exchange to find vehicles and manage a purchase.

TRADEX. links these participants through vehicle information, the dealer portal and the teams that handle the trade.

Who trades with us

Vehicle manufacturers (OEMs)

If you are bringing a group of vehicles to market, share the models, expected volumes and availability with our purchasing team. Both sides can then assess whether the stock fits the exchange.

TRADEX. supplies professional dealers across Europe. An initial discussion can identify where your stock fits the types of vehicles dealers seek for resale.

  • Models
  • Volumes
  • Availability

Rental companies

Fleet renewal can make several vehicles available at once. TRADEX. buys vehicle packages from rental companies. Tell our purchasing team which models you expect to release, in what volumes and when.

For vehicles we buy, our teams coordinate preparation at the compound and onward transport. Vehicle information helps dealers decide what fits their market.

  • Fleet renewal
  • Release dates

Vehicle suppliers

We buy packages of young used vehicles from leasing companies, fleet operators, importers and dealer groups for resale to professional dealers across Europe. A stock list, available vehicle records and an indication of timing help our purchasing team assess the package.

Dealers can then assess individual vehicles against their assortment and local demand.

  • Stock list
  • Vehicle records
  • Timing

Dealers

Compare model, mileage and vehicle details with the cars your customers are looking for. Check the delivered price before booking to see how the vehicle, transport and export fee affect your purchase calculation. VAT is excluded.

Save vehicles in Watchlist or set a target price in Price watch. Your account manager can help with vehicle selection or an open purchase.

  • Watchlist
  • Price watch

Professional buyers

Eligible businesses can apply for access to the dealer portal. Once approved, buyers can review delivered prices, book vehicles and follow the payment instructions in their account.

Follow the purchase and transport by VIN, and access documents and invoices in the same place. Your account manager can answer questions about a purchase or delivery.

Purchasing

From selection to delivery

How buying works.

The dealer portal gives approved buyers a place to find vehicles, see the delivered price and follow each booking. Save vehicles in Watchlist or set a target price with Price watch when you are still deciding what to buy.

New customers can apply through the existing registration route. Approved customers can sign in with their dealer account.

  1. 01

    Select and book vehicles

    Check the vehicle details and delivered price first. When you have found the right vehicles, review your selection and book it in the dealer portal.

  2. 02

    Review payment instructions

    Open the booking in your account and follow the payment instructions. Your account manager can answer questions about the booking or payment process.

  3. 03

    Follow the purchase and transport

    Use the vehicle identification number (VIN) to follow the individual purchase. The portal shows purchase and transport status, and the logistics team arranges delivery to the agreed destination.

  4. 04

    Receive vehicles and documents

    Find the purchase documents and invoices in the same account. You can speak to your account manager about the delivery or use the portal to submit a claim.

Coming in 2027.

Planned for early 2027

Payment terms of up to 60 days

Longer payment terms are planned from early 2027 for eligible dealers. The proposed terms would allow up to 60 days to pay for vehicle purchases. Eligibility and conditions are still being defined.

Planned service

Trading as a service

The planned service would let suppliers ask TRADEX. to coordinate the sale of vehicles on their behalf, including finding buyers, transport and documents. It is a proposed addition to our current purchase of vehicle packages.
