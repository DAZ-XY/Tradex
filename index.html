THE POWER OF EXCHANGE.

EUROPE'S CAR TRADING ECOSYSTEM.

TRADEX. connects professional car dealers and vehicle suppliers across Europe.

We purchase young, high-quality used vehicles from leasing, fleet and rental companies, importers and dealer groups, then supply them to professional dealers across European markets.

Our team handles sourcing, pricing, vehicle preparation, transport and documentation using our own infrastructure. In the dealer portal, you can manage purchases, transport updates, documents and invoices in one account, with a dedicated account manager.
