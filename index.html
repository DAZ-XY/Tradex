What we do today

TRADEX. is a European B2B vehicle trading company. We source and buy packages of young used vehicles from leasing and rental companies, fleet operators, importers and dealer groups, making them available to professional dealers across Europe.

Our teams coordinate the agreed transaction, from vehicle preparation and loading to transport and documentation. After booking, the Dealer Portal brings status updates, documents and invoices into one place.

Why the Ecosystem exists

Suppliers need a reliable route to market when vehicle stock becomes available. Dealers need vehicles that fit local demand, with a delivered price they can assess before booking.

Buying in packages connects available stock with professional demand. Suppliers have a direct purchasing contact, while dealers can select individual vehicles suited to their market.

What we are building

We are developing a connected trading Ecosystem that brings vehicle information, commercial decisions and operational workflows together. Vehicle data and delivered pricing already support buying decisions; the Dealer Portal helps customers follow their purchases.

Our own compound and workshop support vehicle preparation, our logistics team arranges transport and a dedicated account manager supports each customer.

Who the Ecosystem connects

Vehicle manufacturers, rental companies and other suppliers can discuss available stock with our purchasing team. Dealers and professional buyers can source vehicles for their markets.

TRADEX. connects these key players through vehicle information, commercial expertise, the Dealer Portal and the teams responsible for handling each trade.

Vehicle manufacturers (OEMs)

Share available vehicle models, expected volumes and timing with our purchasing team. We assess whether the stock fits professional buying requirements.

TRADEX. supplies professional dealers across Europe, helping match vehicle availability with demand in their local markets.

Rental companies

When renewing a fleet, share the models, volumes and planned release dates with our purchasing team. TRADEX. buys vehicle packages from rental companies.

For vehicles we purchase, our teams coordinate preparation and onward transport. Vehicle information helps dealers assess stock for their market.

  • Fleet renewal
  • Match & Allocate
  • Vehicle packages

Vehicle suppliers

We buy packages of young used vehicles from leasing companies, fleet operators, importers and dealer groups. Share stock lists, available vehicle records and timing so our purchasing team can assess the opportunity.

Individual vehicles are made available to professional dealers, who can assess them against their stock requirements and local demand.

  • Trade & Control
  • Data-driven assessment
  • Remarketing

Dealers

Assess vehicles against your local demand using model, mileage and available vehicle details. Before booking, check the delivered price, which includes the vehicle, transport and export fee, excluding VAT.

Save vehicles in Watchlist or set a target price in Price watch. Your account manager can help with vehicle selection or an open purchase.

  • Watchlist
  • Price watch

Professional buyers

Eligible businesses can apply for access to the Dealer Portal. Find the fast-selling cars on your market, review delivery prices, book vehicles and finalize your payment, in one place.

Follow purchase and transport status by VIN, access documents and invoices and contact your account manager for help with a purchase or delivery.

Planned services

Coming in 2027

We are developing two additions to the Ecosystem. Their timing and conditions are subject to confirmation.

Planned for early 2027

Payment terms of up to 60 days

Longer payment terms are planned from early 2027 for eligible dealers. The proposed terms would allow up to 60 days to pay for vehicle purchases. Eligibility and conditions are still being defined.

Planned service

Trading as a service

The planned service would let suppliers ask TRADEX. to coordinate the sale of vehicles on their behalf, including finding buyers, transport and documents. It is a proposed addition to our current purchase of vehicle packages.
